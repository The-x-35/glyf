**How expected value (EV) is computed here.** EV = payout × my rough estimate of Glyf's win probability. The probabilities are my judgement, based on Glyf's record (two partner prizes and a Top 10 at Mumbai), the size of each field and how well Glyf fits. None of them comes from a source. Investment offers are marked as option value, not cash.

## Five weekend builds turn December into Q1 prize eligibility

Sponsor money is now repeatable: the same names (Uniswap, 0G, ENS, Arc, World, Definitive) appear across ETHGlobal events. So the best use of December is to build the integrations those sponsors already paid for, with dated commits and a changelog. Continuity judging needs exactly that: proof of the prior state, a dated log of new work, and judging of new work only ([ETHGlobal Mumbai prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/prizes)).

**ENSv2 name resolution in the Send chips** targets the richest identity prize: $7k open plus $3k for "Best Integration of ENSv2 into an Existing Project" at Mumbai, which required ENSv2 on Ethereum mainnet. **A USDC send or "pay me" chip on Arc** targets Arc's "Best Mobile Web Consumer Agent" ($3k), its Continuity prize ($3k) and its $10k HackMoney pool, and opens Circle's milestone-paid developer grants ([DevGrants Daily](https://devgrantsdaily.com/)). **World IDKit as a "verified human" badge on payment requests** targets $5k open and $2.5k Continuity. **x402 pay-per-answer**, where Glyf's AI pays in USDC for a premium API call, is one add-on that fits the Solana Foundation's Agentic Payments theme, Concordium's agent payments, UBC's agentic payments track and the DoraHacks x402 pattern. **Seed Vault signing on Seeker** meets an explicit Solana Mobile grant criterion and the SKR "best Solana Mobile Stack integration" prize pattern. A sixth, **0G Storage**, strengthens both the Apollo application and 0G's $3k "Keep Building" Continuity track.

These builds also split the work cleanly by chain. On the Solana side, the money comes as grants and an accelerator (India grant, Mobile grant, Eternal, Colosseum). On the EVM side, it comes as sponsor bounties (ETHGlobal online, ETHMumbai, Cannes). Pitch Solana as the product's core and EVM as the bounty surface.

## Conclusion

For a solo founder in this window, writing applications pays better than hacking. A day of grant writing against a $4,822 average Solana India grant beats any weekend event confirmed before late January, because the events that would beat it (ETHGlobal online, a Solana Mobile hackathon) are not announced yet. The real risk is in sequencing, not in missing events. If Apollo's "up to $2M*" turns out to be equity, accepting it before May could cost Glyf the Eternal Award, which bars VC-funded teams, and could move it out of Base Batches' pre-seed bracket. Read Apollo's terms first, and if it is equity, decide which path matters more before signing.

Several findings rest on thin evidence. X/Twitter pages and DoraHacks could not be fetched, so many announcements, including the ETHGlobal 2027 calendar, may already exist and were missed. The Arbitrum buildathon, UBC's online option, Concordium's pool and both India grants' open status are unverified. Every EV figure is a judgement-based estimate. The one action that cannot wait is the Apollo form by 10 November. Then check ethglobal.com/events weekly in November: if the 2027 online event appears, it becomes the most valuable weekend Glyf has in Q1.
