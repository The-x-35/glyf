# Glyf brand kit, for the launch video

Glyf is an AI keyboard for iPhone and Android. It reads what you're typing and offers the next step on the bar above the keys: a rewrite, an answer, a place, a price, a trade. You never leave the chat.

Tagline: **Type less. glyf more.** Descriptor: **The AI keyboard that does the rest.**
Website: tryglyf.com · X: @tryglyf · Updated 12 November 2026.

## What to show, in order

### Lead with (the story of the video)
1. **Rewrite in one tap.** Type `hey are we still on for dinner tmrw`, pause, tap **Tidy this up**, pick a tone (Basic, Professional, Polite, Shorter, with emoji), Insert. Screens 05, 13, 14.
2. **Answers while you type.** Type `whats the capital of australia?`, tap **AI answer**, Insert "Canberra". Screen 06.
3. **Hold space, Glyf does the rest.** Hold the space bar still for half a second and the AI works on the message. Film the thumb close up.
4. **Buy, swap and set orders from the chat.** `$aapl` shows the live price with Buy; Buy opens a swap (Uniswap on Base, Jupiter on Solana). `set a stop-loss on 0.5 eth at 2800` opens a Definitive order; `dca $20 in eth each month` sets a recurring buy. You approve in MetaMask or Phantom; Glyf never holds keys. Screens 08–11.
5. **Places, search and videos inside the keyboard.** `pizza near me` → **Show me places** → a map card with spots → Insert. (Record from tryglyf.com's chat demo or a phone; the simulator doesn't draw maps.)

### Show (one beat each)
6. **Vault**: address, wallet address, notes, images and shortcuts like `;sig`, filled into any field. Screen 15.
7. **Send crypto to a name**: `send 5 usdc to alex.eth` (ENS) or `.sol` (SNS); Glyf shows the address before your wallet signs. New this week; screens to follow.
8. **My glyfs**: hold a letter key to run your own AI action (C Calendar, P Proofread, E Emotion check, J Emoji-fy). Screen 02.
9. **Translate as you type**, with Replace.
10. **Plans and meetings**: "lunch thursday at 1?" offers a calendar event, a Google Meet link or a booking link.
11. **Instant answers on the phone**: `dinner was $90 for the four of us` → **$22.50 each**; sums, units, currencies, city time.
12. **Private payments on Solana by MagicBlock**, in Connected tools. Screen 16.

### Montage (quick cuts, or leave out)
13. A keyboard that learns you: autocorrect, whole-word next suggestions, glide typing, voice typing, QWERTY/QWERTZ/AZERTY/Colemak. Screen 07.
14. Clipboard and Paste chip, emoji board with stickers and GIFs, text styles, themes, Snake and Chomp, throwaway email. Screen 12 (the g menu).

### Leave out
- 0G / Private AI (hackathon only).
- Any Glyf wallet or seed phrase (removed; wallets are MetaMask and Phantom).
- Prices, plans, user numbers; "best", "#1", "download now".
- Region-specific details: use US demo data ($, a US address, chats with "Mom" and "Alex").

## A 40-second cut that works
0–5 s messy text to Mom, the chip slides in · 5–12 s pick Polite, Insert, send · 12–20 s pizza near me, Insert · 20–30 s $aapl → Buy → MetaMask approves · 30–36 s montage (hold space, translate, vault, a letter-key glyf, glide) · 36–40 s end card "Type less. glyf more." iPhone and Android, tryglyf.com.

## Design language
- **Logo**: lower-case "glyf" with a violet dot (#7C5CFF) above the end. Black on light, white on dark; the dot stays violet. Clear space: the height of the "g". Never recolour the letters, move the dot, or put a gradient on the logo. Write "Glyf" in sentences.
- **Colour**: black, white and Apple greys; one violet.
  - Light: Paper #FFFFFF, Mist #F5F5F7, Ink #1D1D1F, Ink 2 #6E6E73.
  - Dark: Night #000000, Card #1C1C1E, Ink #F5F5F7, Ink 2 #A1A1A6.
  - Brand: Glyf violet #7C5CFF, violet on dark #C3B4FF, message blue #0A84FF.
  - AI at work (chip edge only, while the AI thinks): #7C5CFF → #D35CFF → #FF5C8A → #FFB547 → #3CC8FF.
  - Keyboard light: board #D5D8DE, key #FFFFFF, function key #B5BAC3. Dark: board #1F1F22, key #48484C, function key #2E2E32.
- **Type**: Inter (fonts/Inter-Variable.ttf, SIL Open Font License). Display Bold, −5% tracking, tight leading; headings Semibold; body Regular 17 px; captions 13 px. Sentence case, never all caps.
- **Shapes**: pill buttons and chips; app cards with 20–24 px corners; hairlines, almost no shadows. The violet dot marks anything with a glyf on it.
- **Motion**: 200–300 ms ease-out, no bounces. Key callouts like Apple's; the chip slides in after a pause at a word's end; its gradient edge drifts only while the AI works; cards rise from the bar.
- **Heads-up**: the chip's rainbow edge is being redesigned next; keep chip close-ups short or easy to re-film.

## Voice
Short, plain, a little playful. Say what happens, then stop. Use: "Type less. glyf more.", "The AI keyboard that does the rest.", "Hold space. Glyf does the rest.", "Never leave the chat." Avoid hype, crypto promises, and claims about users or revenue.
