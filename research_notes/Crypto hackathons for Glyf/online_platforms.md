# Online crypto + AI hackathons and bounty platforms for Glyf (Nov 2026 – Mar 2027, excluding ETHGlobal and Colosseum)

Research date: 9 November 2026. About 22 searches and fetches. Read the method caveats in the Gaps sections. DoraHacks (`dorahacks.io/hackathon` and its API) returned HTTP 405 to the fetcher. Akindo's wave-hacks page is JS-rendered and listed no events. Encode's homepage rendered empty. Rise In's earn page was truncated. Listings for those platforms are therefore only partly covered, through search snippets. The Devpost public API (`devpost.com/api/hackathons`) did work and is the most reliable source below.

Labels: **[OPEN]** confirmed open in the window. **[CLOSING ≤7d]** deadline within a week of 9 Nov. **[PREV EDITION]** a past date, useful only as a pattern. **[UNCONFIRMED]** a lead that was not verified on a primary page.

## Which of these allow existing projects / continuing builds

### Takeaway
Continuing builds are explicitly rewarded by Akindo WaveHacks (grants per wave), Stellar SCF (grant), Gitcoin (dormant) and the Base Batches Startup Track. Most Devpost and DoraHacks hackathons need new work made during the event period. Each set of rules still needs checking.

### Cited Findings
- Akindo waves pay grants as teams "build and submit across successive waves". — [Rise In / Akindo](https://www.risein.com/midnight/akindo-buildathon); [Akindo Substack](https://akindo.substack.com/p/beyond-hackathons-from-builder-to)
- Stellar SCF is a grant fund of up to $150K in XLM, with applications by invite after an interest form. — [HackList](https://hacklist.io/)
- Base Batches has a Startup Track (an accelerator) distinct from its Student Track. — [Devfolio](https://base-batches-startup-track-3.devfolio.co/)

### Inferences
- The highest expected value for a solo founder with a live product is probably Akindo waves and the Base Batches Startup Track, plus sponsor-specific Devpost or DoraHacks prizes where Glyf's existing integrations (0G, Uniswap, Solana) count.

### Gaps
- The existing-project rules for Kiroween, PayPal AI, Airwallex and Encode Concordium were not verified.
- No official X/Twitter URLs were verified for DoraHacks, Devpost, HackQuest, Taikai, Lablab, Gitcoin, Circle, Chainlink or Airwallex. Only @encodeclub, @akindo_io and @base appeared in the sources.
