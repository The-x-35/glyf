# India crypto/web3 hackathons, bounties and grants for Glyf (window: 9 Nov 2026 to Mar 2027)

Research date: 9 Nov 2026. Big picture: India's Q4-2026 crypto calendar was pulled into early November around Devcon 8 Mumbai (IBW 1–2 Nov, ETHIndia stage 4 Nov, Monad Blitz 4 Nov, Zcash Free Society 4–5 Nov, ETHGlobal Mumbai 5–7/6–8 Nov). Most of the big India events in the window have therefore **already happened**. What remains: rolling India-only grants (Superteam/Solana Foundation), global online programs that Indians can enter (Colosseum Eternal, Dev3pack, HackCanton, Concordium), small college hackathons with web3 tracks, and a likely-but-unannounced ETHMumbai 2027 (March).

## Superteam India: hackathons, Solana India events, India-only bounties and grants

### Takeaway
The strongest India-specific money is rolling: the **Solana Foundation India Grant via Superteam India (up to $10k USDG, India only)** and the **Solana Foundation × CoinDCX India Instagrant (up to $15k USDC, India only)**. Both pages show mixed open/paused/closed status, so confirm before applying. No Superteam India hackathon was found in the window. Solana Summit India (2 Nov) is past.

### Cited Findings
- **Solana Foundation India Grants (by Superteam India):** up to 10k USDG; "only open for people in India"; focus areas include consumer apps, payments/stablecoins, mobile, tokenization/RWA, dev tooling; average grant $4,822; 127 recipients, $612.4k approved; about 30-day response. The page shows both "Open" and "Applications Paused" — [Superteam Earn listing](https://superteam.fun/earn/grants/solana-foundation-india-grants/)
- Projects are generally expected to open-source after mainnet, and high-reputation Superteam India members get priority — [Superteam Earn via search snippet](https://superteam.fun/earn/grants/solana-foundation-india-grants/)
- **Solana Foundation & CoinDCX India Grants (Instagrant):** up to 15,000 USDC (range $1–$15k), paid monthly to an Okto wallet, India only; focus includes mobile (Android apps on Solana), tokenization/RWA, loyalty/creator monetization, financial inclusion; average grant $3,547; 173 recipients, $613.6k approved. The listing is marked "Closed" but still shows an "Apply Now" button — [Superteam Earn listing](https://superteam.fun/earn/grants/Solana-fdn-coindcx-instagrant)
- Solana India Fellowship (2026): 8 weeks, $2.5k stipend for the top 20 fellows (a stipend, not a grant). The cohort timing in the window is unknown — [Superteam India on X](https://x.com/SuperteamIN/status/2025218511015162208)
- Superteam backs "agentic builders with up to $200" for MVPs built with "solana new" (global microgrant) — [Superteam on X](https://x.com/superteam/status/2043008853525303773)
- Solana Summit India was 2 Nov 2026 (PAST); Superteam India's Nov calendar shows only a Poker Night (online, social) — [Superteam Luma](https://luma.com/superteam), search summary of [Superteam India X](https://x.com/superteamIn)
- Superteam Earn India region page did not render listings (JS app); live bounties need checking in a browser — [superteam.fun/earn/regions/india](https://superteam.fun/earn/regions/india)

### Inferences
- Glyf fit: Glyf is a consumer **mobile** Solana app (Phantom deeplinks, Jupiter swaps, xStocks, MagicBlock private payments, payment requests in chat), which matches the "consumer apps, payments/stablecoins, mobile" focus of both India grants. Realistic ask: about $5–10k (averages are $3.5–4.8k).
- Superteam India sidetracks are usually attached to Colosseum hackathons (see below). Watch Superteam Earn for an India sidetrack on the next Colosseum main hackathon (likely spring 2027).

## Global online programs open to Indian solo founders (Nov 2026–Mar 2027)

### Takeaway
The highest-value item in the window is **Colosseum Eternal** ($25k award, accelerator path to $250k; existing projects allowed if materially changed; start a sprint by 20 Mar 2027). Smaller online ones: Dev3pack (students), HackCanton S4, Concordium Trusted Agents.

### Cited Findings
- **Colosseum Eternal:** self-run 4-week sprint; open Nov–Mar; "start your sprint by March 20, 2027"; Eternal Award **$25,000 USDC** about every 6 months (winners picked in May and Oct); standout teams may get the **$250K pre-seed** accelerator (Solana required for accelerator funding; any chain can win the Award); no VC raised for the startup; existing projects allowed if materially changed (pivot, months of progress, traction); weekly 1-minute videos; no geographic restriction stated — [colosseum.com/eternal](https://colosseum.com/eternal)
- Crypto World's Fair (Colosseum, 14 Sep–12 Oct 2026, $800k+ prizes) is in judging (past for entries) — [colosseum.com](https://colosseum.com/), [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/colosseum-crypto-worlds-fair-hackathon/)
- **Dev3pack Global Hackathon:** 27–29 Nov 2026, online or local build station; Solana; for **students**; $10,000+ for Best App Overall plus Solana Mobile awards; open source; teams up to 4 — [hack.dev3pack.xyz](https://hack.dev3pack.xyz/), [web3voyager](https://web3voyager.com/blog/web3-hackathons-november-2026)
- **HackCanton Season 4:** online, 12 Nov–16 Dec 2026 (projects due 6 Dec); RWAs/DeFi/business workflows; prize not stated — [web3voyager](https://web3voyager.com/blog/web3-hackathons-november-2026)
- **Concordium Trusted Agents Hackathon (Encode Club):** online, 2 Nov–9 Dec 2026; agent identity and **agent payments**; prize not stated — [web3voyager](https://web3voyager.com/blog/web3-hackathons-november-2026)
- Midnight Hackathon (MLH): online, 13–15 Nov 2026, consumer data privacy apps, prize TBA — [web3voyager](https://web3voyager.com/blog/web3-hackathons-november-2026)
- UBC 2026 Hackathon: hybrid/online plus UT Austin, 13–21 Nov 2026, $23k+, tracks Agentic Payments & AI (agent wallets), Consumer Apps — [web3voyager](https://web3voyager.com/blog/web3-hackathons-november-2026)

### Inferences
- **Glyf fit:** Eternal is the best match. Glyf is a materially changed, shipping consumer Solana mobile app (wallet, Jupiter swaps, xStocks, MagicBlock private payments, chat payment requests). A sprint started Nov–Feb could chase the May 2027 award and an accelerator interview. Watch for a Superteam India sidetrack on the next Colosseum main hackathon (spring 2026's Frontier ran 6 Apr–11 May, so spring 2027 is likely, though unconfirmed; ref [Colosseum Frontier](https://colosseum.com/frontier)).
- Concordium "agent payments" and UBC "agentic payments / consumer" fit Glyf's /act + wallet flows, but need the user to check prize sizes and whether UBC's online entry is open to non-US teams.
