## Colosseum: next global hackathon, Eternal, accelerator, existing-project rules

### Takeaway
No Colosseum global hackathon is confirmed for Nov 2026 – Mar 2027. Crypto World's Fair (14 Sep – 12 Oct 2026) is in judging, and on past patterns the next main hackathon would be around Apr–May 2027 (unconfirmed). The opportunity that is actually open in the window is **Colosseum Eternal**. It is a rolling 4-week online sprint, and its Nov–Mar window has winners picked in May. It offers a $25k USDC Eternal Award and consideration for the accelerator's $250k pre-seed. Previous or existing products are allowed if they have materially changed and the team has not raised VC.

### Cited Findings
- **Colosseum Eternal is open now.** The homepage says "Eternal is open: start a 4-week sprint" and that hackathon winners can "join Colosseum's accelerator with $250,000 in funding" — [colosseum.com](https://colosseum.com)
- **Eternal terms:**
  - You start the sprint yourself from the dashboard. It runs 4 weeks, with a one-minute progress video due each week.
  - The final submission needs: product name and description, team background, GitHub repo, pitch video and technical demo.
  - Eternal Award: **$25,000 non-dilutive USDC** to the most innovative submission each semi-annual cycle.
  - The timeline shows Eternal windows **Nov–Mar (winners chosen in May)** and **Jun–Aug (winners in October)**. No exact dates are given.
  - Standout startups may be considered for **$250,000 pre-seed** and the next accelerator cohort.
  - The accelerator is 12 weeks, hybrid: 2 weeks in San Francisco, 10 weeks remote. A decision comes within 14 days of submission.
  - Any chain may enter, but accelerator funding requires Solana. A team that has raised VC for the startup cannot win the award. One sprint per cycle. No country restrictions are listed.
  - A product from a previous Colosseum hackathon qualifies only if it has "materially changed" (pivot, substantial progress over months, or meaningful traction). Minor updates do not count.
  - Source: [colosseum.com/eternal](https://colosseum.com/eternal)
- **Eternal reopened in late July 2026.** Posts from Solana Developers ("Eternal is live again") and community accounts repeat $250k funding, a slot in the next accelerator batch and the $25k Eternal Award — [Solana Developers on X](https://x.com/solana_devs/status/2081808330616340640); [SolanaSensei on X](https://x.com/SolanaSensei/status/2082116122179154182). One aggregator lists odd figures ($26K award / $25K pre-seed), which look like a typo — [search summary of hunted.space/phdeck pages](https://hunted.space/dashboard/colosseum-eternal)
- **Main hackathons in 2026:**
  - Frontier (Spring 2026): **6 Apr – 11 May 2026**, online. Grand Champion $30,000, Public Goods Award $10,000, University Award $10,000, and 20 standout teams at $10,000 each (about $250k in total). Accepted accelerator teams get **$250,000 pre-seed** — [colosseum.com/frontier](https://colosseum.com/frontier)
  - Crypto World's Fair (Fall 2026): **14 Sep – 12 Oct 2026**, online, open to every chain. Prizes are reported as **$800K+ prizes and $2.5M venture funding**. Its organisation into partner-ecosystem tracks plus a general pool is described in [Expanding the Arena](https://blog.colosseum.com/expanding-the-arena/) — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/colosseum-crypto-worlds-fair-hackathon/). Another outlet says "over $3.3 million in prizes and investments" (likely prizes plus funding combined) — [ua.news](https://ua.news/en/ukraine/solana-colosseum-vidkriv-khakaton-crypto-worlds-fair-dlia-proiektiv-na-vsikh-blokcheinakh-prizi-perevishchat-3-3-mln)
  - The homepage currently shows "Crypto World's Fair (Judging)" — [colosseum.com](https://colosseum.com)
- **Hackathon history:** Frontier (Spring 2026, 2,858 projects), Cypherpunk (Fall 2025), Breakout (Spring 2025), Radar (Fall 2024), Renaissance (Spring 2024). No 2027 dates are listed. Winners are announced "roughly one month after the submission deadline" — [colosseum.com/hackathon](https://colosseum.com/hackathon)
- **Existing-project rule (main hackathons):** the hackathons are for new startups, not "established companies that have been building the same product for years and have already raised venture funding". Teams may start development before the event, but only work done between the start and end dates is judged. Pre-existing code is allowed if all past development is disclosed — [colosseum.com/hackathon](https://colosseum.com/hackathon)
- **Colosseum blog, latest posts (27 Aug – 6 Nov 2026):** only weekly "Codex" posts. The 22 Oct post covers Seeker Connect. No new hackathon or cohort is announced for Nov 2026 – Mar 2027 — [blog.colosseum.com](https://blog.colosseum.com)
- **Previous pattern:** Cypherpunk winners were announced at Breakpoint on 12 Dec 2025 — [Colosseum on X](https://x.com/colosseum/status/1995933186040398145)
- **Other 2026 event:** Solana/Colosseum also ran a first AI Agent Hackathon, **2–12 Feb 2026**, with $100,000 USDC in prizes (PAST; a similar early-2027 edition is unconfirmed) — [Genfinity](https://genfinity.io/2026/02/02/solana-colosseum-launches-ai-agent-hackathon/)
- **Links:** X [x.com/colosseum](https://x.com/colosseum); arena and Eternal at [colosseum.com/eternal](https://colosseum.com/eternal).

### Inferences
- **Eternal is the best fit for Glyf in the window.** It is online, has no country restriction, and accepts an existing product (Glyf has never entered a Colosseum hackathon, so the "materially changed" test only matters if it had). It has a $25k cash award plus a chance at the $250k accelerator. Glyf must not have raised VC; prize winnings are not VC.
  - Pitch angle: "AI keyboard as a consumer Solana wallet surface". Cover Phantom/MWA signing, Jupiter swaps and xStocks, MagicBlock private payments, and the Seeker dApp Store build.
  - Timing: start a sprint in Nov–Feb so it ends inside the Nov–Mar window.
- **The next Colosseum main hackathon is probably April–May 2027**, based on the spring Renaissance/Breakout/Frontier cadence. It is likely announced around Feb–Mar 2027 and would fall mostly outside the window. Not confirmed.
- **Main-hackathon catch:** only work done during the event is judged, so Glyf would need a clearly new Solana feature built inside the dates, with prior work disclosed.
