## ETHGlobal: events Nov 2026 to Mar 2027, Continuity Track, and the sponsor prizes that fit Glyf

### Takeaway
ETHGlobal's own events page lists **no hackathon between Mumbai (5–7 Nov 2026) and Cannes (14–16 Apr 2027)**. Nothing in-person or online is listed for Jan–Mar 2027. The only realistic ETHGlobal entry in the window is an unannounced online/async event early in 2027, following the HackMoney pattern (HackMoney 2026 ran 30 Jan–11 Feb 2026 with $75k). Watch for the 2027 calendar, which a sponsor proposal says will be announced in November 2026. Mumbai showed that the Continuity Track is now a standard ETHGlobal feature, with existing-project prizes from Uniswap, 0G, ENS, World, Arc, 1inch and others.

## Base / Coinbase: Base Batches, Builder Grants, Builder Rewards, CDP bounties

### Takeaway
Base Batches 004 (Fall 2026) is **closed**: applications ran 19 Aug–9 Sep 2026, the cohort was announced 3 Nov, and Demo Day is in NYC on 17 Nov 2026. Batches 005 has not been announced. Base's other money is retroactive and rolling: Builder Grants of 1–5 ETH by nomination, and weekly Builder Rewards via Talent Protocol (about 2 ETH/week, current terms unconfirmed). Glyf's Base tokenized-stocks and swap features fit a published Base "Request for Builders: Tokenized Stocks".

### Cited Findings
- Base Batches 004 offers $100K investment from the Base Ecosystem Fund (subject to diligence) and an 8-week virtual program, ending with Demo Day in New York in Nov 2026. Eligibility: pre-seed (angels allowed, no formal seed round), Base as the default network (multichain OK), focused on trading, payments, agents or financing. Apply at batches.base.org — [Base blog: Introducing Base Batches 004](https://www.base.org/blog/introducing-base-batches-004)
- Batches 004 applications closed 9 Sep 2026 (some listings say 10 Sep), acceptances were due 17 Sep, and Demo Day is 17 Nov 2026 in NYC — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/base-batches-004-investing-1m-startups/); [ZHC Institute](https://www.zhcinstitute.com/grants/base-batches-004/)
- Cohort of 10 teams announced 3 Nov 2026 — [Base blog: cohort](https://www.base.org/blog/base-batches-004-cohort); [Base on X](https://x.com/base/status/2090115624764629058)
- Batches 003 drew 1,175 applicants in February (2026). This suggests a spring cohort pattern, so a Batches 005 call in about Feb 2027 is possible but UNCONFIRMED — [KuCoin news summary](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/base-batches-004-accelerator-opens-applications-for-100-000-investment-offers)
- The Base blog lists related posts "Request for Builders: Tokenized Stocks" and "Request for Builders: Funding the Future of Global Finance" (no amounts) — [Base blog](https://www.base.org/blog/introducing-base-batches-004)
- Base Builder Grants are retroactive, 1–5 ETH per recipient, with 20+ cohorts since Mar 2024. Recipients are found by discovery and nomination, not an open form (secondary sources) — [dtso.org Get Funded on Base](https://www.dtso.org/startups/get-funded-on-base); [rocknblock grants list](https://rocknblock.io/blog/blockchain-ecosystem-grants-list)
- Builder Rewards (Talent Protocol) pay weekly ETH to active Base builders. One source says 2 ETH/week. Older tiered split: top 10 share 1 ETH, next 25 share 0.5 ETH, next 65 share 0.5 ETH. Current 2026 terms are UNCONFIRMED — [Talent Protocol on Paragraph](https://paragraph.com/@talent/introducing-builder-rewards-weekly-eth-for-the-most-impactful-builders-on-base)
- Base Creator Grants of up to $4,000 are for creators, not builders — [EGamers](https://egamers.io/base-launches-creator-grants-of-up-to-4000-seven-months-after-killing-its-token-rewards/)
- Base X: [x.com/base](https://x.com/base)

### Inferences
- Glyf's AAPLc/tokenized-stock buys and Uniswap swaps on Base are a direct match for the "Tokenized Stocks" Request for Builders. Glyf would qualify for Batches only if it is pre-seed and treats Base as its default chain. The next realistic shot is a Batches 005 call, likely in early 2027 (unconfirmed).
- Builder Rewards and Grants need public onchain activity and GitHub on Base, so Glyf would need Base-attributed transactions (builder codes or contract activity) to rank.

### Gaps
- No Coinbase Developer Platform bounty program in the window was found.
- No "Onchain Summer"-style Base campaign is announced for Nov 2026–Mar 2027.
- Batches 005 dates are unknown.
