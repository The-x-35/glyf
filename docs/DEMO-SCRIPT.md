# Glyf demo video: the script (ETHGlobal Mumbai)

**Target length:** 3:30, which leaves room under the 4:00 limit. Stay between 2 and 4 minutes.

**Rules:** your own voice, nothing sped up, 720p or more, no music with captions, and no AI voice.

## Setup (before you press record)

- **Record on the Mac.** Plug the iPhone in, then QuickTime › New Movie Recording, and choose the iPhone as the camera. Pick an external mic if you have one. ETHGlobal rejects videos recorded on a phone.
- **Glyf build:** the one installed on 6 Nov evening. Turn on Private AI on 0G (Profile › Keyboard).
- **Wallets:**
  - **Base:** a few dollars of ETH in the Glyf wallet, or in MetaMask with Sign with set to MetaMask.
  - **Solana:** about $2 of SOL in Phantom.
- **Open a Messages thread** with a friend's name. Clear the drafts.
- **Do Not Disturb on,** battery above 50%, time near 9:41 if you can.
- **Do a run-through.** Cut any waiting in the edit (cutting is allowed; speeding up isn't).

Times are cumulative. Words in quotes are what you say. ⌨️ is what you type and 👆 is what you tap.

## 0:00–0:20 · What Glyf is

On screen: Messages with the Glyf keyboard up.

> "This is Glyf, an AI keyboard for iPhone. It's already on TestFlight: it answers, rewrites, plans and searches right inside any chat. At ETHGlobal Mumbai we taught it to trade, privately, from the words you already type."

## 0:20–1:15 · Definitive Flash: advanced orders from a sentence

⌨️ `sell 0.002 eth if it drops below 2000`

> "I just typed a stop-loss. Glyf reads it on the phone, with no AI call, and Definitive Flash quotes it right in the keyboard."

👆 The Flash card, then **Place**.
- **With MetaMask:** MetaMask opens, you approve, and it comes back.

> "Glyf builds the order on the phone; my wallet only signs. It checks the signature and the transaction before Flash gets them."

On screen: the placed order, then Profile › Wallet › Ethereum › Orders.

> "Every Flash order type works this way: take-profit, limit, brackets, trailing stops, even DCA: buy twenty dollars of ETH every day for a week."

⌨️ `sell 0.02 sol if it drops below 100`

> "And on Solana, Phantom signs. Same sentence, different chain."

👆 **Place**. Phantom opens, you approve the setup and sign the order, then you're back.

## 1:15–1:35 · Social: share and copy a trade

👆 **Share in chat**, back in Messages, then the strip's **Share trade**, then send.

> "Trades are social. I share mine into the chat as a link, and my friend taps Copy this trade. It opens the same order in their Glyf, and nothing happens until they confirm."

On screen: the link opened, showing the **Copy this trade** page. Open it on a second phone or in Safari.

## 1:35–2:00 · Uniswap: swaps from any chat

⌨️ `swap 0.002 eth to usdc on base`

> "Swaps go to Uniswap: a live quote in the keyboard, and one tap."

👆 **Swap**, then confirm. Show the Basescan link.

> "Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and Unichain."

## 2:00–2:30 · 0G: the keyboard's AI, private

Type a casual message: ⌨️ `ya ok will send the deck before 6 tmrw`. Hold **space** and pick a rewrite.

> "Everything I type passes through a keyboard, so its AI has to be private. On this build every AI call runs on 0G's Private Computer, inside a secure enclave. Names, card numbers and codes are swapped out on the phone before it sends anything."

On screen: Profile › Keyboard › 0G receipts.

> "And it's checkable: each answer has 0G's request id, verified by 0G."

## 2:30–3:15 · Glyf's own features

Pick the three that look best in the run-through.

**Calendar:**
- ⌨️ `lunch tomorrow at 1?`
- 👆 the plan chip, then **New event**.

> "Plans become calendar events, with a Google Meet link when I want one."

**YouTube and Maps:**
- 👆 the **g** key, then **YouTube**, then search `lofi beats`, then tap a result. The link and title land in the chat.

> "Search, YouTube and Maps, without leaving the chat."

**Build your own:**
- 👆 Glyf app › Build, then **Send a new Meet link**, then **Save**.
- Back in Messages, hold the key you bound it to.

> "And anyone can build their own glyf. It can use my calendar, my location or a fresh meeting link, with a switch for each tool."

**Typing:** only if time is left.
- ⌨️ `I love you ` and the learned ❤️❤️❤️ comes up.

> "It even learns how I type, emoji included."

## 3:15–3:30 · Close

> "Glyf: a keyboard that places Definitive Flash orders, swaps on Uniswap, and thinks privately on 0G. All of it from the chat you're already in. Type less, Glyf more."

- **A quote is slow:** cut the wait in the edit. Don't speed it up.
