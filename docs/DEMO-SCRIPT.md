# Glyf demo video: the script (ETHGlobal Mumbai)

**Length:** about 2:55 (the limit is 2 to 4 minutes). About 360 spoken words, at an easy pace. Your own voice, nothing sped up, 720p or more, no music.

## Before you record

- **Record on the Mac:** plug in the iPhone, open QuickTime › New Movie Recording, and pick the iPhone as the camera.
- **Wallet:** about $25 of USDC and $3 of ETH (for gas) on Base, in the Glyf wallet.
- **Keyboard:** **g** shows Uniswap, Definitive and 0G.
- **Phone:** Do Not Disturb on, a Messages thread open.
- **Edit:** do one run-through. Cut waiting in the edit; cutting is allowed, speeding up isn't.

⌨️ is what you type, 👆 is what you tap, and the quotes are what you say.

---

## 0:00–0:15 · What Glyf is

On screen: Messages, with the Glyf keyboard up.

> "This is Glyf, an AI keyboard that replaces your phone's keyboard. Everything you'd leave the chat for, AI and on-chain, happens right here."

## 0:15–0:45 · Stocks on-chain, through Uniswap

⌨️ `$apple`, then 👆 **Buy** on the chip. The trade panel quotes $10 of USDC into Apple on Base. 👆 **Buy**, then sign, then show the Basescan link.

> "I type $apple. That's Apple stock, tokenized on Base, and Uniswap quotes it right in the keyboard. One tap, and I own a piece of Apple, on-chain."

## 0:45–1:20 · Orders that wait, through Definitive Flash

⌨️ `buy $10 of tesla if it dips below 300`, then 👆 **Place**.

> "Orders that wait go to Definitive Flash. This one buys Tesla only if it drops below 300."

⌨️ `buy $5 of eth every hour for 2 hours`, then 👆 **Place**.

> "And this is a DCA: five dollars of ETH every hour. Stop-losses, take-profits, brackets and trailing stops work the same way. I type it, my own wallet signs it, and Glyf never holds my keys."

## 1:20–1:50 · Private AI on 0G

⌨️ `ya ok will send the deck before 6 tmrw`, then hold **space**, then 👆 **Professional**.

> "A keyboard sees everything you type, so its AI has to be private. Glyf's AI runs on 0G's Private Computer, inside a secure enclave, and every answer comes with 0G's verified receipt."

⌨️ `dca 20$ in eth each month`, then show the chip with the Flash plan filled in.

> "The same AI works out what I mean and offers the right action."

## 1:50–2:35 · Everything else, one tap each

Keep each one to a few seconds.

- **Calendar:** ⌨️ `let's meet at 5pm`, then 👆 the plan chip, then Add.
  > "It spots plans and puts them in my calendar."
- **Temp mail:** 👆 **g**, then **Temp mail**, and show the inbox.
  > "A throwaway email for sign-ups, with its inbox right here."
- **YouTube:** 👆 **g**, then Search, then YouTube, search `lofi`, and send it.
  > "YouTube, Maps and search, without leaving the chat."
- **The menu:** 👆 **g** and pan across Clipboard and Games.
  > "Clipboard, games, and more."
- **Build your own:** open Glyf › Build, then **Send a new Meet link**, then Save.
  > "And I can build my own actions, like a fresh Meet link on one key."

## 2:35–2:50 · It's yours

Profile › Wallet: the balance, Ethereum and Solana, and Apple now in the wallet.

> "It learns how I type, and my wallet shows everything I hold."

## 2:50–2:58 · Close

> "Glyf: stocks and swaps on Uniswap, orders on Definitive Flash, private AI on 0G, all from the keyboard. Type less, Glyf more."

## If something fails on camera

- **An order is too small:** use $10 or more.
- **A quote is slow:** cut the wait in the edit.
- **No funds:** record up to the quote, say "with funds this places", and cut to an earlier take.

## After recording

1. Export at 1080p and check the length (under 3:00).
2. Upload it on the ETHGlobal dashboard under Project › Video.
3. Post it on X and Discord tagging **@DefinitiveFi**. Definitive requires this for its prizes.
