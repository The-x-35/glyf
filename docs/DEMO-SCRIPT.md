# Glyf demo video: the script (ETHGlobal Mumbai)

**Length:** about 2:45 (the limit is 2 to 4 minutes). Your own voice, nothing sped up, 720p or more, no music.

## Before you record

- **Record on the Mac:** plug in the iPhone, open QuickTime › New Movie Recording, and pick the iPhone as the camera. ETHGlobal rejects videos recorded on a phone.
- **Glyf:** Profile › Keyboard has Private AI on 0G turned on. In the keyboard, **g** shows Uniswap, Definitive and 0G.
- **Funds:** about $20 of ETH on Base (Glyf wallet or MetaMask) and $10 of SOL in Phantom.
- **Prices on 7 Nov:** ETH about $2,240 and SOL about $90. Keep stop-losses below today's price.
- **Phone:** a Messages thread with a friend, Do Not Disturb on, battery above 50%.
- **Edit:** do one run-through. Cut any waiting in the edit; cutting is allowed, speeding up isn't.

⌨️ is what you type, 👆 is what you tap, and the quotes are what you say.

## If something fails on camera

- **An order is too small:** Flash says it's below the minimum. Use $10 or more.
- **A wallet has no funds:** record up to the quote card and say "with funds, this places", then cut to an order from an earlier take.

## After recording

1. Export at 1080p and check the length.
2. Upload it on the ETHGlobal dashboard under Project › Video.
3. Post it on X and Discord tagging **@DefinitiveFi**. Definitive requires this for its prizes.
