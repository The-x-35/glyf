# Working agreements

How Arpit wants Glyf built. These rules come from his direct instructions and from his reactions when something went wrong, with his words quoted. Anyone working on the project, human or AI, should follow them.

---

## 1. Pace, scope and communication

- **Move fast and finish end to end.** "we dont have 16 -22 days, we building everything today only", "finish everything end to end and cleanly", "finish fast". Half-built features are not done.
- **Verify by using it.** "i need all features properly for both ios and android and verify them too by using". Test on the simulator, the emulator and in the browser, then say plainly what was verified and what wasn't.
- **Change only what was asked, in the direction asked.** Two incidents to never repeat:
  - Asked only to remove the app icon's background, the letter and dot were changed too: "i just fucking asked u to remove bg and u asshole change dot possition and everything".
  - Asked to shrink the gap between the mobile form and keyboard, the text was pushed down instead of the keyboard coming up: "this looks more bad now ... i wanted keyboard to go up a bit and be bigger on mobile".
- **Ask on real design calls; research everything else.** "u can ask me wherever u are ambiguous instead of taking shit design decisions". Also: "study moree and research more before making anything". When keys or accounts are needed, ask directly: "so fucking ask me what apis u need from google and how to get it and i will give".
- **Keep his preferences when he says he liked something.** "i liked the previous key list more", "why u remove made for thumbs i love that line", "even the previous one was better ig with heading and list below", "coffee script is perfect".
- **Commit and push only when Arpit asks.** The repo is `The-x-35/glyf` (private).
- **No AI traces in the repo.** No co-author or "generated by" lines in commits, no assistant config folders committed, and no AI tool names in docs, apart from Arpit's own quoted words. See D89 in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
- **Document decisions.** "note down everything in docs". Keep [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md), [TIMELINE.md](TIMELINE.md) and the area docs current when things change.

## 2. Design language

- **Apple first.** "study apple's design language i need simplistic designs like that", "see apple's design lang and make". Clean, flat, lots of white or black, weight-600 type, one short line per idea, the product shown large.
- **No explainer lines. Anywhere.** "i dont like explainer lines everywhere remove them", "i hate explainer lines they look shit", "i dont need explainer lines anywhere". Use headlines and functional labels only, in the apps and on the site.
- **No em dashes** on the website (and in these docs). "they look fucking disgusting ai made". Use commas, periods, colons or "·".
- **Brand:**
  - The accent `#7C5CFF` appears only as a dot or small highlight: never purple washes and never borders on keys.
  - The wordmark is "glyf" with the dot raised at the top right after the f, the same on every surface.
  - The app icon is the keycap "g" with its dot, on white.
- **Glyf's own look, never a copy.**
  - Acti is a reference for features and polish, not a template ("you do not need to copy it", "dont blindly copy").
  - Gboard is the reference for keyboard UX (grid menus, search, themes).
  - iMessage is the reference for the site's chat.
- **Same look on both platforms.** "i want exactly same features in all , and ios style in both".
- **Smooth.** "i need smooth as fuck experience". Micro-interactions matter ("a very important part which is micro interactions and animations").
- **Premium.** "its good rn but nor premium, make it premium".
- **Light and dark.** On the site now, and in the apps later ("i need both light and dark modes both in site and later in apps too").
- **The website doesn't copy the mobile app's UI.** "i like ui ux on website dont copy mobile for that, its shit in mobile rn".

## 3. Product rules

- **Things must actually work.** No hardcoded or mock results shown to users: "you have hard coded results in ios idiot the fuck make them work". Mock mode is for automated tests only.
- **Never make a website visitor type.** The keyboard types for them; they hold, tap and send. "u cant ask user to type soo much just make hiim do actions".
- **Guide, don't lecture.** Teach features by making people need them (a friend asks for your address, you open the vault), with a coach ring and pill, not text.
- **Real assets and real numbers.** Use the official SOL, USDC and MagicBlock marks. Show real counts (dictionary size, installs), never invented creators or download numbers.
- **The keyboard knows you.** Name, address, UPI, wallet and socials are one tap away ("u dont need to fucking remember or go and copy paste from other apps").
- **Local language matters.** Hinglish autocorrect must leave "yaar", "haan", "kal" and "bhai" alone; India starts with the Hinglish pack.

## 4. Engineering rules

- **iOS keyboard extension:** native Swift only. Flutter never links into it, and CI checks this.
- **Secrets:**
  - They live only in `server/.env` (and `site/.env.local` for the site). Never in client bundles, git, docs or chat output.
  - Keys belong in env files, not pasted in chat.
  - Never print the Neon connection string. Rotate it before launch, because it was pasted in chat.
  - Don't reuse the exposed YouTube key for billed APIs; regenerate it after deploy.
- **Identifiers:** don't change the App Group, Keychain, MethodChannel or logger names of a shipped app (it would wipe user data on update). Since the move to Arpit's own account (2026-09-25) the iOS bundle IDs are `com.arpit.glyf` and `com.arpit.glyf.keyboard` and the App Group is `group.com.arpit.glyf`; Android is still `com.demo.glyf`.
- **Updates keep user data.** The vault, notes and settings must survive app updates.
- **Production wiring:** release builds point at `https://api.tryglyf.com`, and debug builds at local servers.
- **Website builds:** run `next build` in a copy of `site/`, not in place, or the dev server loses its CSS.
- **Git hygiene:** no `.env` files, no build output, no files over 100 MB, no assistant config folders.
- **A push to `main` is a production deploy.** Vercel is connected to the GitHub repo and redeploys www.tryglyf.com on every push. Verify builds before pushing site changes.

## 5. Tools and permissions

- **No broad filesystem scans.** "the fuck are u requesting to access everything the fuck u need". Use known paths (Flutter tooling, `/opt/homebrew/share/android-commandlinetools/platform-tools/adb`) or ask.
- **Don't trigger a stream of permission prompts.** When looking at other websites, just open, screenshot and read them. No JavaScript inside other sites, no headless Chrome scripts, no background browsing agents. "why are u asking for perms so much access site on browser and see idiot tf u executing js".
- **Use the attached simulator and emulator** rather than asking for wider machine access.
- **Mobbin** needs Arpit to authorize its connector. **Paper** is installed, but its free plan has a weekly MCP limit.

## 6. Privacy and money

- **Vault and typing data stay on the device.** Learned words stay on the phone and out of cloud backups.
- **AI privacy isn't being worked on yet.** "we will not work on ai privacy rn as we will have privacy policy". Known caveat: Gemini's free tier trains on what's sent and excludes apps likely used by under-18s.
- **No credit card for now.** "i dont wanna add my card here rn". Prefer free, no-card providers (Geoapify, Gemini/Groq free tiers) until Arpit decides otherwise.
- **Never enter passwords or payment details** on Arpit's behalf. Deploys, logins and purchases are his to do; give him exact steps.

## 7. How to report back

- Lead with the result. State what was verified and how, and what wasn't.
- Short and scannable. Plain words, no jargon, no filler.
- When something needs Arpit (a key, a login, a design choice), say exactly what and where.
